# ReVolt AI Website Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Evidence Brutalism
Very high-contrast enterprise neo-brutalism: hard rules, off-register blocks, oversized type, and a teal signal color make proof feel physical and unmistakable.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Quiet Infrastructure
A restrained editorial system with pale paper surfaces, technical diagrams, and narrow typography; calm, precise, and designed like a compliance report that learned product design.
Probability: 0.03

### Theme Name: Signal / Custody
A dark, data-dense control-room aesthetic with amber and teal indicators, serialized asset records, and kinetic scanning motifs.
Probability: 0.09

## Chosen direction: Evidence Brutalism

### Design Movement
Neo-brutalism translated for enterprise infrastructure: visible structure, honest materials, blunt hierarchy, and purposeful friction. The visual language borrows from industrial labels, early web utility, and modern climate-tech dashboards without becoming retro.

### Core Principles
1. **Proof over polish.** Every visual treatment should make a workflow, status, or evidence relationship clearer.
2. **High contrast, low ambiguity.** Use ink-black, bone, and one ownable teal accent with hard borders and offset shadows.
3. **Physical-to-digital translation.** Pair kiosk, device, serial, and custody motifs with software-like timelines and evidence records.
4. **Asymmetry with discipline.** Use offset compositions, split rails, and irregular blocks while preserving a strict spacing rhythm.

### Color Philosophy
Ink (#101313) carries the seriousness of security and audit. Bone (#F3F0E8) gives the system a paper-record warmth rather than a sterile SaaS white. Signal teal (#18D7C1) is reserved for verified states, active workflow steps, and the ReVolt mark so that proof reads as a visible event. Acid yellow (#E8F05A) is a sparing annotation color for pilot-phase callouts and attention, not a decorative gradient.

### Layout Paradigm
A left-anchored reading rail with a persistent vertical section index, full-bleed dark bands, and offset content slabs. The page should feel assembled from evidence boards and industrial labels instead of centered marketing modules.

### Signature Elements
- Oversized black-on-bone headlines with compressed line breaks.
- Teal evidence stamps, serial-number labels, and hard-edged cards with offset shadows.
- A living custody timeline that converts a device into a digital record.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like operating a trustworthy system: explicit, immediate, and reversible. Buttons press into their offset shadows, timeline events reveal metadata, and cards change state through color and label—not through ornamental effects.

### Animation
Use quick 140–220ms transitions with a physical ease-out. On hover, cards translate 3px and release their shadow; the scanning line traverses the simulated asset record once on entry; timeline nodes reveal in 40ms staggered steps. Avoid perpetual motion except for the smallest status indicator. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Use **Space Grotesk** for display and interface labels, with **DM Mono** for serials, timestamps, and evidence metadata. Headlines are uppercase, tightly tracked, and allowed to wrap aggressively. Body copy uses Space Grotesk at a relaxed line-height. Mono is never used for paragraphs.

### Brand Essence
ReVolt AI is the evidence infrastructure for retired enterprise hardware—built for IT, security, compliance, and sustainability teams that need proof after the device leaves the desk. Personality: **precise, unflinching, constructive**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and diagnostic. CTAs sound like an operational next step, never a hype promise. Microcopy labels what is known, simulated, planned, or still in validation.

Example lines:
- “Retired hardware is still a live risk.”
- “Make the last mile auditable.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the supplied ReVolt AI mark as the primary reference where available. The supporting symbol system is a compact split-arrow / circuit notch that suggests a device entering a controlled loop; it must work as a bold symbol without text and remain legible at favicon size.

### Signature Brand Color
Signal teal: **#18D7C1**. It is the only color that denotes a verified event, active state, or ReVolt-owned evidence layer.

## Content Guardrails

All customer, revenue, certification, partnership, pilot, capacity, and performance claims remain grounded in the supplied pitch deck, revenue model, and brief. Simulated asset records are clearly labeled as demo interfaces. Future capabilities are labeled Prototype, Planned, Pilot phase, Target, or Validation objective where appropriate. No testimonials or fabricated customer proof are used.

## Style Decisions

- Full-section backgrounds default to ink or bone. Signal teal is reserved for verified and active evidence elements and primary actions; acid yellow appears as a pilot or annotation signal rather than a decorative field.
- Every major section exposes a left-side evidence rail with section number, status label, or serial metadata so the page reads as one continuous custody record.
- The ReVolt split-arrow/circuit-notch symbol recurs as an owned evidence stamp across the header, evidence timeline, and footer.
